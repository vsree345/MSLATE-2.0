      The unit of measure
      
            
      Every threshold is expressed in the 1-minute expected move, derived from India VIX at the open:
      
            
      EM = PDH × (VIX / 100) × √(1 / 94 500) × 1.5
      
          
      
          
      
            
      Workflow strategies
      
            
      S1 PDL breaks, an up LIS candle > 2× EM confirms the bottom, the next bar holds → initial call.
               S2 price retests a post-entry LIS high within 1.5× EM → pyramid.
               S3 after 12:30, two wick rejections at the high plus ITM put selling → put.
               S4 after 12:30, low tags the Fib 50% with a fresh up LIS → call.
      
          
      
          
      
            
      Fake or real?
      
            
      Every PDH/PDL break starts pending — don’t chase. It turns fake if price closes back inside by half an expected move within ten bars, and real after five closes beyond, a 3× EM extension, or no reclaim in time. S5 mirrors S1 for a fake break of the previous day’s high.
      
          
      
          
      
            
      Nothing trades below 70
      
            
      Strategies only propose. Each proposal is scored on six checks over the setup window — futures displacement 30, option ladder 25, volume 20, delta acceleration 10, trap structure 10, regime 5. 80+ trades full size, 70–79 half size, anything lower is logged as gated.
      
          
      
          
      
            
      Trade and risk management
      
            
      Size comes from risk: 1% of equity per full-size trade, never more than 2%. Stops are gap-aware, the target is 2R, the stop trails 1.5× EM behind the best price once a trade is +1R, and everything is squared off at 15:15. A 2.5% daily loss flattens the book and ends the day. P&L is index points × lot size — a delta-1 proxy that ignores option premium, theta and IV.
      
          
      
          
      
            
      AI second opinion
      
            
      Every 15 bars, and whenever a setup is scored, a brief of the last 30 candles, levels, flow and the engine’s view goes to Gemini — or headless Claude Code if Gemini is unavailable — which answers lean buy, lean sell or wait with a confidence and reasons. It runs off the main loop, keeps only the newest brief when busy, and never blocks or vetoes a trade.
